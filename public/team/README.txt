Team photos for the landing page
=================================
Drop four images in this folder with these exact names:

  jed.jpg       Jed Neo Mirabueno
  jorence.jpg   Jorence Perualila
  michael.jpg   Michael John Quinones
  john.jpg      John Michael Dela Cruz

Square images work best — around 400x400. They're displayed as circles,
so anything important should sit in the middle.

.png works too: change the filename in the TEAM array near the top of
src/app/page.tsx to match.

Any card without a photo falls back to the person's initials, so the
section still looks finished while you're collecting them.
